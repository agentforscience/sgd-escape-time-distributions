\para{Deviations from the plan.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt,topsep=0pt]
    \item We replaced the planned short quench with the converged quench, before production, for the reason in \secref{sec:method}.
    \item We added the barrier-or-loss rule after the Newton audit and reran the 10 affected runs with the same seeds.
    \item We moved the base condition from $\eta=0.2$ to $\eta=0.1$, because at $\eta=0.2$ escape competes with in-well relaxation. We kept $\eta=0.2$ as the hot condition.
    \item We made \fmnist at weight decay $10^{-4}$ the main landscape, because weight decay made \digitsdata minima nearly uniform in sharpness.
    \item We used a smaller network for saddles, because mode-following Newton did not converge at 290 parameters. We rejected symmetric saddles, because per-sample gradients vanish along their escape directions.
    \item We saved but did not analyse the secondary loss-level and radius first-passage definitions, and we analysed quench-length and exit-rule robustness instead.
    \item 165 of 166 jobs finished. The missing job is default-learning-rate Adam at the sharpest minimum.
    \item We did not fix the H4 radius in advance, so we report all three radii and all three noise levels.
\end{itemize}
